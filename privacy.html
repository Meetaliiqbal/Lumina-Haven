Introduction

At Lumina Haven, accessible from luminahaven, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Lumina Haven and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We may collect standard log files and analytics data when you visit our website. This information may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is used for analyzing trends, administering the site, and tracking user movement on the website.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Lumina Haven uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content.

Third-Party Partners & Advertisers

Some of our partners and partner networks may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Our advertising and CPA partner networks have their own privacy policies for their user data handling. For easier navigation, we encourage you to review their respective privacy statements.
